# NANCYFLIX v7 — fixes and owner notes

## What changed
- Marquee rebuilt as a two-column bar: the scrolling track lives in its own clipped viewport, so it can never run under the NOW tag.
- All emoji removed from the interface and replaced with a built-in SVG icon set (film, live, ball, ticket, download, bell, lock, shield, crown, table, calendar, arrows).
- Live TV cards rebuilt with a fixed grid: name, plan badge, one description, live state and source. No absolutely-positioned badges, so nothing overlaps on narrow screens.
- Trailer player back button is now a solid dark pill with a white label on every theme.
- Founder accounts (bridah888@gmail.com, agollanancy@gmail.com) automatically activate the Super plan for testing; other accounts keep normal limits. Founder badge is the highest rank (gold + red). Members get a verified badge that levels from 1 to 5 with watchlist saves, watch history and account age.
- East African line-up expanded: Citizen, Inooro, K24, KTN News, KTN Home, NTV, KBC, Ramogi, Kameme, TV47, 3 Stones, Akili Kids, Kass, Meru, Mashariki (KE), Bukedde 1/2 (UG), TBC 1/2 (TZ), plus France 24, DW and Al Jazeera.
- Free now includes Citizen TV, Inooro TV, K24 and France 24, so a free account has working Kenyan feeds even when Citizen is between bulletins.
- Football no longer relies on the rate-limited free key: full 2026/27 fixtures come from the open football database (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1), and tables are calculated from played results.
- Channel player supports direct HLS feeds with an automatic YouTube fallback; source buttons switch between them.

## Honest limits
- YouTube live embeds only show a picture while the broadcaster is actually live. Citizen and the KTN/NTV channels are not 24/7 on YouTube; the player explains this instead of faking a stream.
- `api/live.js` can resolve the current live video server-side after deployment; without it the app falls back to the channel embed.
- Direct HLS feeds are public broadcaster streams and can move or go offline without notice.
- Fixtures cover five major European leagues. Champions League data is not in the open dataset and is not shown rather than showing a single fake line.
